## Scientific decisions for the repair

The worker's reading of `sign_decision` is consistent with the original
[CollecTRI paper](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/51/20/10934/7318114):
signs come from references, then regulon-majority inference, with activation
assigned by default for remaining interactions. The paper's resource also
includes co-regulatory and general transcription factors, not just DNA-binding
TFs.

- Retain the primary exclusion of default-activation weights as an explicit
  project analysis choice; retain the assigned-weight sensitivity output. Do
  not claim this filtered policy is the standard CollecTRI benchmark or proven
  more accurate. No model comparison campaign is needed.
- Rename `evidenced_sign_only` to an accurate policy label such as
  `literature_or_regulon_sign`, consistently in code, artifacts, verification,
  and documentation. Regulon-inferred signs are not direct edge-specific
  experimental evidence. Keep raw weights and sign-basis provenance.
- Individual-gene CollecTRI regulators may remain candidates; a second TF census
  is not required. Describe this scope explicitly in output documentation so
  later reports cannot imply every candidate binds DNA directly. Do not infer
  direct binding from a CollecTRI regulator entry or a high ULM score.
- Keep `min_targets=5`, signs, and observed-target coverage visible. Small target
  sets warrant cautious interpretation, not an invented confidence threshold.
