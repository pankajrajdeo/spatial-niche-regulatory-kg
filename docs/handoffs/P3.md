**Pool rule.** An association enters the pool only if all of the following hold:
- the bundle is `READY_FULL_TEXT` and has a `source_result_candidate` attribution, so abstract-summary bundles are excluded;
- the TF of that association, judged per TF, is consistent with a source identifier, so a resolved co-mentioned TF never carries it in;
- its context label is not background/resource or irrelevant;
- the anchor passage itself names the lineage's cell: strictly (the manuscript population) or broadly (its cell class, e.g. macrophage or fibroblast), using the ontology-backed `source_context`.

**Order.** Picks go round-robin across lineages. Within a lineage the preference order is: a TF new to that lineage, then stronger context tier, then a strict cell mention, then a named regulator, then retrieval priority.

### Corrections implemented (items 1–7)

1. **Per-paper screening** (`p3-screen-1`, `workflows/corpus_ready.py: screen`).
   - Every candidate-TF mention (validated queue names and aliases) in every acquired document gets a ledger row: `screening_ledger.parquet/.csv`.
   - Each row records the publication, document and version, source asset SHA-256, locator/page (or table region with row locators and match count), matched TF and terms, every manuscript lineage association with its context label, attribution, outcome, reason, rule, and processing status.
   - Supplement table rows are grouped into table regions and marked `requires_table_interpretation_review` (2,486); they are never bundled.
   - Methods mentions are retained as supporting context (377). Titles and reference sections are recorded as not bundled.
   - BM25 plus cached embeddings only order candidates (`prioritize`); they never exclude one.
   - No positive-result keyword is required. Negation and comparison cues are flags only.
2. **Context labels** (`p3-context-3`, `literature/screening.py`).
   - Source-reported species, lung tissue (strict), other tissue, disease, and manuscript cell-type phrases (strict vs broad) are recorded per paper, separately from the query/lineage association.
   - Provisional relevance per paper × lineage is one of: `direct_context_candidate`, `potentially_transferable_mechanism`, `biological_background_resource`, `irrelevant_to_assigned_question`, or `unresolved`.
   - "Alveolar" counts as lung only with lung anatomy ("alveolar bone" is dental). Lung fibroblasts are grouping-level only.
   - Experimental attribution (`p3-attribution-1`) comes from section and citation markers. Introduction/discussion or cited statements stay `attribution_unresolved`: they are never attributed to the citing paper and never ready.
3. **Serialized limits and readiness** (`p3-serialize-1`, `p3-corpus-payload-2`).
   - `serialize_evidence()` is the single measurement for both the limit and the budget. It counts text, cells, header paths, locators, and the mention records for manuscript genes; the count of other mentions is kept.
   - Hard limits: ≤ 3 parts and ≤ 6,000 characters. Nothing is truncated. An oversized anchor is `serialized_size_exceeds_limit` (84), and required context that does not fit is `context_not_included` (373).
   - A following passage that continues the anchor ("However, …") is linked context even without the TF name.
   - Blocking conditions: missing abstract-only disposition; partial parse (the missing regions are listed); retraction; unresolved attribution; ambiguous TF identity; required supplement unavailable.
   - A source-identifier conflict on the TF (for example a mouse ortholog) stays a visible flag.
   - `source_bundles_ready.jsonl` is kept separate from `context_investigations.jsonl`.
4. **Supplement dependencies** (`p3-dependency-1`, `supplement_dependencies.csv`).
   - Every cited supplementary item of a candidate bundle is resolved, in evidence order, by JATS label, the article's own JATS caption (for example "Figures S1–S7"), or a filename that encodes kind and number. Each has its own resolution label.
   - Single or generic containers stay `…_container_unverified`. Unresolved references are never matched by proximity.
   - Only needed open-access files were acquired: 56 needed, 55 acquired (166.6 MB, MD5-checked), 1 deferred over 30 MB. They were parsed through the existing routes.

     - required evidence assets: 75 rows / 75 papers (72 supplements, 3 article PDFs);
     - potentially useful, awaiting relevance review: 62;
     - lower-priority background: 110.
   - Access restriction (`access_restricted_or_not_open_access`) is reported apart from transient failures.

## Limitations and blockers

No blocker. For review:

1. **The corpus is dominated by lung cancer, not AT1 or IPF biology.** Eight of 10 papers are NSCLC/SCLC/LUAD; one is alveolar epithelial senescence in mouse fibrosis (PMID 38945958). That is what 20 bounded queries and exact-TF eligibility yielded.
   - KLF5 is covered only by a lung genomics paper mentioning KLF5 in its abstract; no returned lung paper names KLF5 in its title.
   - Seed coverage means an exact mention, not relevant evidence.
2. **Search asymmetry.** PubMed `[tiab]` phrase queries are strict (0 hits for most assay queries), while Europe PMC's default fields include OA full text and are broad. The source translations are recorded per query in `queries.parquet`.
3. **Europe PMC reliability.** It gives intermittent version-only replies and HTTP 500 from `fullTextXML` (for non-OA articles, and for OA PMID 27158780). Both are handled as failures; PMID 27158780 uses PubTator BioC instead, whose tables are flattened with no header row.
4. **612 PubTator mentions (25%) are unaligned.** They carry external offsets only, so downstream grounding must use aligned spans. Since the repair, exact candidate-name hits are retrieval hints only and do not establish identity (R1).
5. **`CANDIDATE_PASSAGES_FOUND` means an exact TF mention in a retrieved passage.** It does not imply a target mention, relevance to AT1, or support.
6. **Selection-rule iterations.** Rules 1–2 were superseded during development (decision 4). Only rule 3's output is kept; the heuristics are provisional and documented.
7. **Not done, per scope:** no PDF processing (deferred), no PubTator relation search, no reranker or model comparison, and no chat-model, P4, or Neo4j work.
8. **README** was not updated, as it is outside P3's allowed writes. The CLI help documents the new commands.
