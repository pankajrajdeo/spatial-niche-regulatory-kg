# Navigate your inbox and calendar in a flash, with an AI-powered search assistant for emails

Superhuman’s Ask AI product has users declaring: “I can’t live without it!”

Searching through the 45,873 emails in your inbox and finding yourself unable to recall the right keyword or fumbling with Gmail tags is an all-too-common frustration for busy people who spend their days in email and calendars. [Superhuman](http://superhuman.com) set out to solve this challenge with Ask AI, its AI-powered search assistant. Designed to transform how users navigate their inboxes and calendars, Ask AI delivers instant, context-aware answers to even the most complex queries – such as “When did I meet the founder of that series A startup for lunch?”
